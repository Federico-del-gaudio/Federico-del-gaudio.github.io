My name is Federico del Gaudio. I embarked on my journey first attending a computer science high school, and then an academy where I learned the fundamentals of game design, which I am now ready to expand upon.

Thanks to my technical skills, I can bridge the gap between design and direct engine implementation, supporting both designers and programmers. I love both single-player and multiplayer games, competitive and casual alike, and I am deeply passionate about medieval history.
